About

A path that took a few turns to find its footing.

From a top-ranked high school class, through a Musical Theatre conservatory in New York, to Economics and Business Analytics at the University of Houston Honors College.

Biography

From the stage to the classroom.

Jackson Bateman is an Economics student in the Honors College at the University of Houston, pairing a B.B.A. in Economics with a minor in Business Analytics. He's built a working résumé alongside his coursework — managing a retail storefront, waiting tables, teaching kids to code, and currently interning in commercial 3D spatial digitization.

He graduated from Cinco Ranch High School with a 4.45 weighted GPA, ranked in the top 10% of a class of nearly 800, and posted a 1310 on the PSAT (97th percentile) as a sophomore — the kind of academic foundation that's carried through everything since, including a detour into the performing arts.

The Path

How he got here.

2018 – 2022

Cinco Ranch High School

Graduated with a 4.45 weighted GPA, ranked 75th of 793 students, on a course load heavy with AP Calculus, Statistics, Physics, Computer Science, and U.S. & World History. Also ran cross country and performed in school theatre.

AMDA · New York

Musical Theatre training

Trained in acting, voice, and dance at the American Musical and Dramatic Academy, taking on lead roles including Danny Zuko in Grease and Raoul in The Phantom of the Opera, and performing at venues including The Green Room 42 and Broadway in Bryant Park.

Transfer pursuit

Setting a course for economics

Weeks from completing his AMDA training, Jackson made the decision to pursue a transfer to NYU to study Economics. University policy prohibited enrolling full-time at two institutions at once, so he withdrew from AMDA ahead of the transfer — a deliberate trade of one goal for another he wanted more.

Present

University of Houston, Honors College

After the NYU transfer was complicated by a medical issue, Jackson enrolled at the University of Houston's Honors College, where he's now pursuing a B.B.A. in Economics with a minor in Business Analytics.
