About Jackson

Born for the spotlight, trained for excellence.

Jackson is a multifaceted performing artist and AMDA graduate whose vocal ability — a tenor with perfect pitch — sets him apart in both musical theatre and concert performance. His starring roles as Danny Zuko in Grease and Raoul in The Phantom of the Opera showcase his ability to embody iconic characters while bringing a fresh interpretation to each one.

When he's not composing original music or dazzling audiences with his piano skills, you might catch him teaching gravity a lesson with his juggling acts or bringing characters to life through his arsenal of voice impressions. This isn't just talent — this is artistry in motion.
