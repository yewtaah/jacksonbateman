About Jackson

Discipline first, direction found.

Jackson is an Economics student in the Honors College at the University of Houston, where he's pairing rigorous coursework with a minor in Business Analytics. His path here wasn't a straight line — it ran through a top-ranked high school class, a Musical Theatre BFA program at AMDA in New York, and a run at transferring to NYU before landing at U of H.

Along the way he's managed a retail storefront, waited tables, taught kids to code, and is now interning in 3D spatial digitization — the kind of range that comes from actually doing the work, not just reading about it.
